Privacy Policy

Shorts Factory · Effective 8 October 2026 · Operated by Mehmet Akdogan

Shorts Factory is an open-source program that renders short vertical videos on the user's own computer and, if the user chooses, uploads them to the user's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services. This policy explains what data the program uses and what happens to it.

YouTube API Services

Shorts Factory uses YouTube API Services. By using its upload feature you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

When you connect your Google account, the program asks for permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). With that permission it sends the videos you rendered, together with the title, description, tags and scheduled publishing time that you wrote, to your channel. It does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers, messages or any other account data.

Where data is stored

The program runs only on your computer. The OAuth access token Google issues is saved in a file on your computer (youtube_token.json) so you do not have to sign in for every upload. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's. The developer operates no server and never receives your videos, tokens or account data.

Sharing

Data is shared only with Google/YouTube, and only to perform the uploads you start. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke Shorts Factory's access at any time on your Google account permissions page. To delete the stored token, delete youtube_token.json from the program folder. Deleting the program folder removes all data it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
